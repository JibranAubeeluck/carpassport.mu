Buying a used car in Mauritius?

Five checks that protect you before any money changes hands.

  1. Look up the plate

    Confirm road tax, fitness and insurance are current, and see any accident, flood or write-off flags.

  2. Match the chassis number

    Compare the chassis / VIN stamped on the car with the registration book and this record. Any mismatch is a stop sign.

  3. Check the import story

    For reconditioned imports, the JEVIC pre-shipment certificate records the odometer at export — later readings should only go up.

  4. Get an independent survey

    An accredited surveyor grades the condition from A to F and photographs the evidence.

  5. Transfer it properly

    Complete the change of ownership with the NLTA, and ask the seller to transfer the record so its history follows the car.

Questions, answered

How the public record works, and what stays private.

Is the public lookup free?

Yes. Anyone can check a vehicle's road tax, fitness and insurance status, import verification and history flags at no cost. Lookups are rate-limited to stop bulk harvesting of records.

Why are the chassis and engine numbers partly hidden?

Full identifiers can be misused to clone a vehicle's identity. The public sees enough to compare against the car in front of them; owners, and institutions under agreement, see them in full.

Where does the information come from?

Each fact is compiled from its source — registration data, customs and JEVIC import records, insurer feeds and accredited surveyors — and shown with the date it was reported. Self-declared items are labelled as such.

Can I see who owned the car before?

Not publicly. Owner identities are personal data under the Data Protection Act 2017. The owner can see the ownership timeline for their own vehicle and share it with a buyer.

Something on a record is wrong. What do I do?

Contact us with the plate and what needs correcting. We check the source and, if it is confirmed, the record is amended with a dated note — history is corrected, never silently erased.

How do I check a printed report is genuine?

Every report carries a credential such as CP-6K2M-4T9X-2026. Enter it on Verify report to confirm it was issued by carpassport.

About us

The vehicle record of truth for Mauritius.

carpassport gives every vehicle a permanent, verifiable record — from the moment it is imported through its whole life on Mauritian roads — so buyers, owners, insurers and banks can all rely on the same source of truth.

What we do

We keep a single, trusted record for each vehicle: its identity and import history, its road-tax, fitness and insurance status, its condition grade and valuation, and any accident, flood, fire or write-off history. The public can confirm a vehicle is legitimate, owners hold their full record, and accredited insurers and banks verify it under agreement. What each party sees is governed by strict, privacy-by-design tiers.

Who we are

We come from Mauritius's motor-survey and automotive trade — independent surveyors and technicians who have spent years assessing vehicles, and who have seen first-hand how little a buyer really knows at the point of sale. carpassport is built alongside CarPro, our network of trusted service providers, and grounded in a published condition-grading standard applied by accredited surveyors.

Our mission

To close the information gap in the used-vehicle market — giving every vehicle a trustworthy, permanent record that protects buyers from hidden history, lets honest owners prove a vehicle's worth, and gives insurers and banks a shared, fraud-resistant source of truth.

Our vision

To become the recognised vehicle record of truth for Mauritius — the verification layer that dealerships, insurers, banks and, in time, the transport authorities rely on, extending the chain of custody that begins at the border and never breaks.
